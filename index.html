Headliner

8th Day

Live in Concert · Disney-Featured Artists

The internationally renowned Jewish band fronted by brothers Shmueli & Bentzi Marcus, whose electrifying sound and soul-stirring lyrics have captivated audiences around the world.

Eli Lebowicz

Comedian

Eli Lebowicz is a very Jewish comedian. Like very Jewish. He has performed all over the US, Canada, and Israel telling jokes to Jews at shul dinners, Passover programs and other times when they’re eating. He regularly opens for Elon Gold and was featured in the Chosen Comedy Festival in 2023.

Eli is also one of the co-creators of J-Sketch, the Orthodox Jewish world’s recent viral sketch group. You have likely seen some of these sketches forwarded to a WhatsApp group you’re in.

The Lebovics Brothers Choir

Choir

Raised in South Florida, the Lebovics brothers come from a long line of cantors stretching back to Hungary. They grew up with song — Jewish melodies sung around the family table — and they carry that warmth into every note. We can’t wait to hear them sing together on one stage.
